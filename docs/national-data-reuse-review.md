# National data reuse review — 2026-11-26

## Croatia
The Ministry's official open-data inventory explicitly lists the Court Register, REST API XML/JSON access, daily updates and the Croatian Open Licence as its reuse terms.
- https://mpudt.gov.hr/otvoreni-podaci/24346 (Sudski registar section)
- https://data.gov.hr/otvorena-dozvola
- https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2017_07_67_1577.html

## United Kingdom
Companies House offers the Free Company Data Product as a monthly snapshot of live companies. Its public task and reuse guidance permits reuse of public-register information subject to third-party rights and relevant attribution; Crown-produced material is normally under OGL v3.
- https://download.companieshouse.gov.uk/en_output.html
- https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/companies-house-accreditation-to-information-fair-traders-scheme/public-task-copyright-and-crown-copyright
- https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/

## Publication checklist (not legal approval)
Retain source, snapshot date, licence link, and transformation notice in user-accessible provenance and release manifests. Do not imply public authority endorsement. The licence findings support reuse of the covered public data, but do not independently authorize all raw fields containing personal information, private contacts or third-party rights. Review actual field inventory and privacy/legal basis, correction and removal procedures, operator identity and hosting access rules. Do not embed credentials or private workspace content. The Croatian snapshot is partial; do not claim full coverage. Keep the existing private production release gate until an approved, minimized distribution and both national packages are verified.
